About Us

Paquant is an AI-powered research assistant that transforms natural-language trading ideas into fully structured, production-grade Python code. Built for quants, traders, and researchers, it automates the most time-consuming part of strategy development: turning human insight into clean, reproducible, backtest-ready code.

Paquant streamlines the quant workflow by taking you from idea to model to metrics in one integrated system. It helps you move faster without sacrificing quality or flexibility. Whether you're a solo algo trader or part of a professional team, Paquant enables rapid prototyping with zero boilerplate and complete control.
